A safer routine

Before. During.
After.

Take a moment at each stage. A safe routine protects you, your family, neighbours, animals, and the environment.

01BEFORE USE

Check what you have.

  • Buy from a trusted, licensed seller and keep the original sealed pack.
  • Check the product name, crop, use instructions, expiry date, and warning symbols.
  • Read the full label. Ask a trained local expert if anything is unclear.
  • Keep products away from food, animal feed, children, and water sources.
02WHILE HANDLING

Follow every direction.

  • Use the protective clothing and equipment specified on the label.
  • Never eat, drink, or smoke while handling a product.
  • Follow label directions for mixing, application, weather, and re-entry.
  • Keep people and animals away for the label-specified time.
03AFTER USE

Close the loop safely.

  • Wash and change as directed on the label; keep work clothes separate.
  • Store leftovers securely in the original labelled container.
  • Never reuse containers for food or water. Follow local disposal rules.
  • Keep the product label and purchase record for future reference.
!
IF SOMEONE MAY HAVE BEEN EXPOSED

Get urgent medical help.

Follow the product label’s first-aid directions and contact emergency services or a doctor right away. Take the product container or a clear photo of the label with you, if it is safe to do so.
