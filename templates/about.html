Soil Script is an early-stage hackathon project that brings document understanding, regional language, and voice together for India's farmers.

The idea

Make the next
step make sense.

A packet, a bank letter, or a government notice can carry important information in a difficult format. Soil Script helps bring the key message into everyday language, with the option to listen.

It also brings simple planning prompts and safety reminders into one place, so farmers can ask better questions and speak with local experts with more confidence.

A NOTE ON IMPORTANT DECISIONS

Helpful explanations are a starting point.

AI may omit, misread, or incorrectly explain details. Soil Script is not a substitute for the exact product label, a qualified agriculture professional, your bank, or a government office. Verify names, numbers, dates, dosages, eligibility, and deadlines with the original source or the relevant authority.
